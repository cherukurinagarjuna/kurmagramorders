# Phase 1: Visual Moodboard & Aesthetic Direction

**Project:** Kurmagram Vedic Village (కూర్మగ్రామ వేదిక్ విలేజ్)  
**Living Tradition:** Srikakulam Pen-Kalamkari & Andhra Vedic Agro-Ecology  
**Core Aesthetic:** Handcrafted Andhra farm brand — editorial, warm, tactile, authentic

---

## 1. Material & Chromatic Foundation

The visual texture of Kurmagram is derived entirely from tangible Andhra village craftsmanship and traditional natural dyeing (*Kalamkari*):

```text
[ UNBLEACHED KHADI ]  ->  Rough handloom cotton fiber; soft unprinted paper tone (#FAF6EE).
[ FERMENTED KASI INK ] ->  Jaggery and iron filings steeped in water; matte iron-black (#231F1D).
[ MADDER ROOT (MANJISTHA) ] -> Deep natural brick red; terracotta tiles and temple cloths (#A62B2B).
[ SACRED VAT INDIGO ]  ->  Fermented indigofera leaves; deep nocturnal river blue (#1E3F66).
[ RAW TURMERIC (PASUPU) ] -> Sun-dried mountain turmeric rhizomes; vivid golden amber (#D97706).
[ ANTIQUE TEMPLE BRASS ] -> Sand-cast temple handbells and oil lamps (*Deepalu*); warm gold sheen (#B5944B).
```

---

## 2. Kalamkari Pen-Work & Linework Discipline

Srikakulam Kalamkari (*శ్రీకాకుళం కలంకారీ*) is distinguished by its meticulous, freehand pen (*kalam*) linework:

1. **Consistent 2px Iron-Black Stroke:**
   - Every product icon, border accent, and seal uses a calibrated, uniform 2px stroke width.
   - Zero variable digital pen pressure or generic geometric minimalism.
   - Organic terminal caps reflecting natural ink absorption on woven cotton.
2. **Botanical Bracts & Leaf Tendrils:**
   - S-curving creeper vines inspired by traditional temple wall murals and saree borders.
   - Micro-motifs: Paddy seeds, sesame blossoms, neem leaves, and tulasi sprigs.
3. **The Sacred Kurma Tortoise:**
   - Geometric shell divisions inscribed with traditional lotus (*Padma*) petals and sacred footstep marks (*Sripada*).
   - Signifies foundational stability, ahimsa, and self-contained harmony.

## 3. Photographic Art Direction (Authentic Farm Stills)

No studio backdrops, no glossy stock models, zero generative AI hallucinations:

* **Atmospheric Lighting:** Golden-hour dawn light (6:00 AM – 7:30 AM) filtering through morning mist over the Eastern Ghats.
* **Subjects & Scenes:**
  - Indigenous desi cows grazing in dew-kissed green paddies near thatched bamboo goshala shelters.
  - Srikakulam red clay soil with composted bio-mass and organic mulching.
  - Farmers sun-drying organic sesame and peanuts on unbleached woven mats.
  - The heavy sal-wood pestle turning slowly in the traditional wooden ghani (*చెక్క గానుగ*).
* **Composition:** Editorial asymmetric framing, deep depth-of-field, natural warm chromatic temperature.

---

## 4. Tactile Materiality: Kraft, Brass & Kumkum

* **Kraft Paper Substrates:** Product cards feature a warm kraft-paper backing tone (`#ECE2CE`) that evokes hand-wrapped parcel packaging and rustic market envelopes.
* **Antique Brass Hairlines:** Section breaks and card headers feature 1px–1.5px metallic brass divider rules (`#B5944B`) mimicking handcrafted brass utensils and temple rails.
* **Kumkum & Turmeric Stamps:** Small circular stamps with authentic imperfect edges mark quality declarations and Goshala Trust certifications.
